Project Overview

The Product:

Vitality is a mobile health application designed to empower individuals to track and manage their overall well-being through personalized data collection and insights. The app enables users to monitor key health metrics such as physical activity, sleep quality, and medical symptoms, offering a comprehensive digital diary for daily health tracking. With intuitive visualizations and structured logging tools, Vitality supports both everyday wellness and long-term health management.

The Problem:

  • • Fragmented tracking of health data across multiple apps or paper journals.
  • • Lack of centralized access to personal health records and symptom history.
  • • Difficulty in identifying patterns or communicating health changes effectively to healthcare providers.
  • • Limited awareness of holistic well-being due to isolated data points (e.g., steps, sleep, pain).

Responsabilities:

  • • Conducting user research and usability testing to inform design decisions.
  • • Creating wireframes, high-fidelity prototypes, and interactive UI components using Figma.
  • • Designing responsive and accessible interfaces optimized for iOS devices.
  • • Collaborating with cross-functional teams (product managers, developers) to ensure alignment on UX goals.
  • • Iterating on designs based on user feedback and stakeholder input.
  • • Ensuring visual consistency, brand identity, and adherence to accessibility standards (WCAG).

User Research

To ensure Vitality meets real user needs, I conducted qualitative research with 10 participants, including individuals managing chronic conditions, fitness enthusiasts, and people interested in preventive health. The goal was to understand how people currently track their health, what challenges they face, and what they expect from a digital health companion. I used semi-structured interviews to gather insights into daily habits, pain points, and motivations. Key questions included:

What health aspects do you monitor regularly?

  • How do you currently record symptoms or changes in your body?
  • What frustrates you about existing health apps?
  • How could an app help you feel more in control of your well-being?

Users often forget to log data due to complex or time-consuming interfaces.

Many rely on memory or paper notes, leading to inconsistent tracking.

There's a strong desire for simplicity, visual feedback, and long-term progress insights.

Users want to share their health history with doctors, but lack organized data.

Information Architecture

The app is structured around four core pillars of well-being: physical activity, sleep, medical symptoms, and a personal health journal. This architecture enables users to quickly access relevant data and visualize connections between different aspects of their health.

Component Library

I designed a scalable component system using a calm, accessible color palette (WCAG 2.1 compliant). The library includes modular components for quick health logging, clear data visualization (progress rings, bar charts, stats cards), and consistent UI elements — ensuring a seamless, trustworthy experience across all screens.

Design Process

01

Research & Discovery

Conducted qualitative interviews with 10 users to understand how they track their health and what pain points they experience with existing health apps. Key insights shaped the core structure of Vitality — focusing on simplicity, quick logging, and clear visual feedback.

02

Design & Ideation

Created low-fidelity wireframes and tested multiple concepts to simplify health logging and data visualization. Focused on intuitive navigation, consistent iconography, and a clean hierarchy that supports daily tracking without friction.

03

Prototyping

Built high-fidelity prototypes in Figma. Tested core flows — including dashboard overview, category logging, and registry access — with real users to validate usability and refine interactions.

04

Testing & Validation

Ran usability tests to validate accessibility, flow efficiency, and daily tracking experience. Iterated based on feedback to ensure Vitality feels effortless, trustworthy, and empowering for users managing their well-being
